Step 1 of 5

SOUND

Configure your instrument before playing.

  • Press START ENGINE to activate camera access
  • Choose a Scale (e.g. Pentatonic Minor)
  • Select a Root note (e.g. C)
  • Pick a Wave Type — sawtooth, square, triangle, or sine
Step 2 of 5

CANVAS

Your hands and face are your instrument.

  • Gesture nodes track your hand skeleton in real time
  • Pinch — touch thumb to finger to trigger notes
  • Move your hand along the X axis to change pitch
  • Move along the Y axis to sweep the filter
  • Keys overlay shows the current scale mapping
Step 3 of 5

METERS

Live readouts of your sound and performance.

  • Analysis — waveform + spectrum visualiser
  • Modulators — mouth, smile & brow expression knobs; cutoff & tilt-bend sliders
  • Channels — CH-1 LIVE, CH-2 IDLE, CH-3 CHORD status
Step 4 of 5

TOGGLES

Control what gets rendered over the camera feed.

  • Ghost — faint body outline behind the canvas
  • Skeleton — draw the full hand/body rig
  • Face Filter — enable facial expression tracking overlay
Step 5 of 5

START

Sign on with a gesture.

  • Point your finger at the START button on screen
  • Hold briefly to activate — the engine will go LIVE
  • You're now playing PHEREMIN
