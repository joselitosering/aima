02 — Build Logistics

How It Was Built

One founder, one Claude Pro subscription, seven days — every phase documented honestly, rest days included.

🌀
Phase 01
Core Visual Engine
The kaleidoscope array — the generative, audio-reactive visual core of the entire product. Duration: Day 1.
🎧
Phase 02
Audio Engine Integration
Drone synthesis, binaural / isochronic tone generation, and the internal sound engine driving the visuals. Duration: Day 2.
📡
Phase 03
Broadcast Output & Hardening
OBS-compatible video output plus a full bug-fix pass on the Stage/Controller architecture. Duration: Day 3.
😴
Phase 04
Rest
Deliberate recovery day — no build. Session limits and sustainable pace are part of the honest story, not a footnote. Duration: Day 4.
🔍
Phase 05
Deep Debug Pass
Full stability sweep across the visual engine, audio engine, and broadcast output before anything shipped. Duration: Day 5.
📘
Phase 06
Documentation & Go-to-Market
Full help guide and the marketing brief & advertising plan — positioning, audiences, pricing, roadmap. Duration: Day 6.
🚀
Phase 07
Launch
Ology v1.1 ships free, live, in the browser. Duration: Day 7.

    07 — Roadmap & Investment

    Roadmap as Marketing Arc

    Each version is a launch moment with its own pricing event and audience expansion.

    🆓
    v1 / v1.1 · Beta
    The Instrument
    Free web; Stage + Controller; OBS output. YouTube live + Spotify podcast launch. Story: "a new kind of meditation, born in the open."
    🎨
    v2 · Subscriber
    Sound & Senses Expand
    App store launch, mobile viewer/controller, 3D/VR upgrade, Ology Product Line (Scentsual, Urbl, Devotion). $9.99/mo · $99.99/yr.
    🎛️
    v3 · Creator
    Full Performance Tool
    Complete DJ/VJ live suite, MIDI/DMX, 4K, Creator Store with shared revenue. $14.99/mo · $149/yr · $249 lifetime.
    Mission
    Revenue supports NGO partnerships tackling education, enterprise, and empowerment for poverty-stricken communities in the third world. Customers aren't just buying calm — they're funding kindness.

    v3 Creators upload presets, visual packs, and sound beds to the Subscriber Store. Downloads split revenue between the Creator and AIMA Productions. Therapists earn referral revenue-share on Subscriber conversions.
