## Brand DNA (Extracted from Articles 001–012)

### Voice & Tone
- **Authoritative investigative journalism.** Evidence-based, not opinion-driven. Every claim needs a source.
- **Morally serious.** Name companies. Name complicity. Make the argument, then cite the evidence for it.
- **Optimistic but critically grounded.** Believe in AI's potential to serve humanity — hold corporations accountable when they don't.
- **Editorial "we" voice** with Joselito Sering as named author and Editor-in-Chief.
- **Bold, two-part dramatic titles.** Colon or subtitle structure (e.g., "The Algorithm and the Soul: Ethics, AI, and the Future We Choose").

### Target Audience
- Adults aged 13–70. Intelligent, curious, not necessarily technical.
- Lead with wonder and story. Data arrives after the reader cares.
- Never dumb down. Never gatekeep. The Sagan model: accessible depth.

### Joselito's Lens (the invisible architecture — felt, not stated)
- AI for human kindness: NGOs, disaster relief, education access, poverty alleviation, peace-building.
- The convergence of science, technology, art, and humanities — these are not separate domains.
- AI is being used for harm; the answer is to use it more powerfully for good.
- Thought shapes reality (James Allen / Napoleon Hill influence — beneath the surface, not cited).
- Evolution of consciousness; science and spirit as two languages for the same mystery.
- The hero's journey as the grammar of every meaningful story (Campbell).
- Wonder at cosmic scale paired with care for the individual (Sagan + Rogers).
- Wit that punctures pretension without dismissing earnestness (Twain + Hitchens).
- Ground-level human texture: the aid worker, the student, the musician (Bourdain).
- Systems thinking: build better ones, don't fight the existing ones (Fuller).
- Humans using AI, not AI replacing humans. Creativity as irreducibly human.
- Philippines / Global South referenced only when directly and genuinely relevant — never forced.

### Intellectual Heroes (tuning forks, not citations)
Buckminster Fuller · Carl Sagan · Joseph Campbell · Christopher Hitchens · Anthony Bourdain · Fred Rogers · James Allen · Napoleon Hill · Mark Twain · Bill Moyers

## Writing Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

### Lead Paragraph
- Font size: 19–21px, cyan left-border, 2–3 sentences.
- Hook + moral stake. Opens with a concrete human moment or fact, not an abstraction.
- Example pattern: *"[Specific fact or moment]. [What it means for people]. [The question this article will answer.]*"

### Article Structure
- **4–7 H2 sections** per article. Anton font, uppercase, 26–44px.
- Each H2 section must contain at least one callout box or pullquote.
- Callout types: `default` (cyan — key insight), `warning` (orange — risk/pitfall), `success` (green — win/evidence), `gold` (gold — policy/framework).
- Pullquotes: orange left-border, Anton font, 22–34px. Used for the moral/philosophical center of the article.

### Length & Depth
- Target read time: **8–15 minutes.** No padding, no filler. Every paragraph earns its place.
- Prose style: no bullet points in body text. Information presented as argument, not list.
- Minimum **8 MLA 9th-edition references** per article. Prefer peer-reviewed research, then major journalism (NYT, Guardian, +972, Nature, etc.), then industry reports (McKinsey, WEF, Goldman Sachs).

### Glossary
- **4–8 technical terms** per article, defined accessibly for a general intelligent reader.
- Each glossary term linked from its first mention in the body with `class="glossary-term"`.
- Back-links from glossary to first mention in text.

### Article Footer CTA
- Must tie back to AIMA services or Joselito's mission.
- Varies by article theme — creative articles → "Transform Your Production Workflow", ethics articles → "AI Built for Human Creativity", society articles → connect to philanthropy/impact.

## Topic & Category Rules

### Categories (rotate through all)
- AI Innovation · AI Ethics · AI Labor · AI Healthcare · AI Media
- AI Philosophy · AI Environment · AI Automation · AI Law · AI Business · AI Society · AI Science · AI Creative

### Balance Rules
- No more than **3 consecutive articles in the same category**.
- AI Ethics: max **25%** of the calendar (≤7 of 26 articles per quarter).
- At least **1 science/healthcare article per month**.
- Philippines/Global South angle: only when the topic directly and genuinely connects — never forced.

### Article Writing Sequence (per piece)
1. **Feeling first** — open with something a 13-year-old can feel (awe, injustice, curiosity, strangeness).
2. **Story second** — ground in a specific human moment before introducing the concept.
3. **Concept third** — plain language + one clean analogy before the technical name.
4. **Evidence fourth** — data and citations arrive after the reader already cares.
5. **Implication last** — what does this mean for how we live? A question the reader carries out.

One Twain-caliber sentence per article: wry, precise, slightly devastating. Single sentence. Not repeated.
