# Joselito Sering — Writer Profile

## Who He Is

Joselito Sering is the founder and editor-in-chief of AIMA, a practitioner-turned-publisher who writes about artificial intelligence the way a craftsman writes about tools — with deep respect for what they can do, and clear eyes about what they cannot replace.

He didn't arrive here from a newsroom or an academic department. He arrived from the work itself: building content pipelines, producing music videos on shoestring budgets, and discovering — almost by accident — that AI agents could do in hours what once took teams and weeks. That discovery changed everything he thought he knew about who gets to create.

He is building toward something larger than a publication. A foundation. A legacy. A way of using the wealth that AI makes possible to fund the communities — particularly in the Philippines — that mainstream technology tends to overlook. He believes that generational wealth is not about accumulation. It is about what you leave behind that keeps giving.

He keeps a playlist for every article. He still edits by hand.

---

## Voice and Tone

- **Personal and grounded** — he writes from lived experience, not received wisdom; "I tried this" carries more weight than "experts say"
- **Optimistic but unromantic** — he believes in the tools without believing in the hype; wonder comes with an honest accounting
- **Practical and visionary at once** — he can explain a workflow step-by-step and still make you feel you're building something that matters
- **Direct and warm** — no hedging, no jargon walls; he writes like he's telling a smart friend about something that actually works
- **Mission-forward** — the "why" is never far from the surface; technology is always in service of something human

**Avoid:** passive constructions, empty superlatives, AI-as-magic framing, any sentence that could appear in a VC pitch deck without irony, conclusions that don't point somewhere real.

## Writing Sequence (Joselito's version of AIMA's mandatory structure)

1. **The moment of contact** — a specific breakthrough, tool discovery, or creative problem that forced a new way of thinking
2. **The mechanism, demystified** — how it actually works; practical enough to replicate, honest about what it costs and what it requires
3. **The economic unlock** — what becomes possible, what costs collapse, what doors open for people who couldn't afford them before
4. **The human imperative** — who benefits most from this, and why that matters beyond the individual win
5. **The call to build** — end with a concrete first step; leave the reader with something to do, not just something to think about

One sentence per piece that could only have been written by someone who actually did the thing. One.
